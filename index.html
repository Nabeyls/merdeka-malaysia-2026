❓ FAQ

▸ Where do I submit my challenge proof?

Use the designated submission method stated in the weekly challenge instructions and complete your submission within the required period.

▸ How do I check my points?

Use the Lark Base Tracker button above to view your recorded progress.

▸ Can I earn more than the weekly maximum?

No. Each activity has a defined point cap. Additional submissions beyond the applicable cap do not create additional points.

▸ What happens if my submission is not valid?

It may be rejected or excluded from the point calculation if it does not meet the stated challenge requirements.
